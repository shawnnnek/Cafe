Rooted in campus life

A table for everyone.

The Midnight is a student-minded cafe with a simple purpose: make campus feel a little more like community.

Why the raven?

Our raven is the curious campus companion: observant, clever, and always finding its way back to the nest. The deep violet around it nods to school pride, while warm cream keeps the welcome easy.

We save seats for study groups, serve quick breakfasts before early classes, and make room for the conversations that turn classmates into friends.
